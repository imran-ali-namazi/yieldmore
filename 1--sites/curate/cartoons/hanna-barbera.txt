<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1oDaHRbIDH8" target="_blank">William Hanna and Joseph Barbera</a> were a production duo that made many many popular cartoons from the 50s to the 90s. Some of their creations / productions include (these will be familiar to any kid growing up in the 90s who got to watch the cartoon network):

Bill and Ted's Excellent Adventures
Flintstones
Jetsons
Richie Rich
Scooby Doo
SWAT Kats
The Adams Family
The New Adventures of Captain Planet
The Smurfs
Tom and Jerry Kids
Top Cat
Two Stupid Dogs
Wacky Races
Young Robin Hood
Yogi Bear

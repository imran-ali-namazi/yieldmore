Welcome to Learn YM, a project of yieldmore.org for learning subjects like math, science, history etc. Our purpose is to bring teachers and students together and provide a platform for our teachers to spread their learning. We are in the process of building our own content, but link to other online content when possible.

Our teachers section is for content targeting teachers (by teacher trainers). And we also disseminate information for parents. We hope to build a network of teachers of schools and colleges and engage in collaboration, quality improvements in the education system and sharing resources with the needy.

We are based in Chennai, India and are focusing our efforts there for the moment, but if you are interested in facilitating/coordinating similar activity in any city in the world, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Feel free to contact us by sending a mail to <a href="mailto:%email%?subject=YieldMore Learn Contact" target="_blank">%email%</a> with a slight background and how you can contribute / engage with us. You can also join the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/LearnYM/" target="_blank">Facebook Group</a>.

In time we also wish to provide the tools for teachers to conduct classes online, manage libraries esp for government school students, distribute pamphlets etc.

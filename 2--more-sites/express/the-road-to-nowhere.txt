As I walk upon this road, I see no end, nor a beginning
But still I tread on, as do so many more of my ilk
Looking ahead at the rising sun
Which, at this very moment, is setting at another
Shedding light on these earthen pots
Giving them life, and strength
To hold water within, and dispense to the thirsty
Lasting a full season, only to bid adieu
And be cremated
With a promise to rise again
As does the Sun Lord, in this cycle of life
On this Road to Nowhere

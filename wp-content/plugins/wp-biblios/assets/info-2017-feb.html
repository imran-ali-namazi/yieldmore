What is YieldMore

A place for all humanity to learn and grow, pooling their learning and resources. A website to organise and coordinate this. Anyone with an open mind, peaceful nature and a quest for progress is welcome.

Beliefs

Recreation, Learning / Sharing, Self Expression, Better Quality Relationships, Personal Growth

Values

Peacefulness, Declutter, Deconstruction, Quality, Decentralize and Omission of the Negative.

PR Material

See our poster, flyer and postcard.

Salient Features

  • a web repository of inspirational information and ideas worth learning and sharing.
  • share ideas that improve the quality of life
  • try to solve problems through the ideas we share here
  • a platform to publish selections and summaries from books and entire works
  • a place where contributors can share their body of work and views on a topic.
  • a meeting place of quality minded people whom one can interact with OFFLINE, on social media and on our mailing list.
  • provide curated lists of movies, books etc to improve language and get exposed to multi-cultural thoughts.
  • be inspired to act, in a spirit of kindness and volunteer to help the not so lucky
  • embrace the path of Learn, Heal, Share, Experience/Emote/Express and Yield More
We are always looking for Curators (make selections worth sharing), Contributors (participate in discussions and write new articles or expand their existing ones) and Conveners (coordinate activities). Contact us by mailing Imran (shasa@cselian.com).

What is YieldMore

A place for all humanity to learn and grow, pooling their learning and resources. A website to organise and coordinate this. Anyone with an open mind, peaceful nature and a quest for progress is welcome.

Why YieldMore

problems arise in the 5 circles (Me, We Family and Friends, Work, Work Ecosystems and the Whole World) and along the 5 cycles (FEPSI - Financial, Emotional, Physical, Spiritual and Intelligence). Kaizen is a quality objective that starts with conflict resolution, empowerment and peak performance development. To evolve something worthy for our children to inherit.

How

There are 4 aspects that need attention viz Personal, Professional, Social and Spiritual. We need to Learn, Heal, Share, Experience / Emote / Express, Love and Yield More. Take help, help in return and give to others. For money or for free or as favours. Anonymously or with attribution.

Objectives

  • collaboratively build a website of inspiring content
  • share ideas that improve the quality of life
  • try to solve problems through the ideas we share here
  • encourage people to discuss and summarize works for others
  • be inspired to act, in a spirit of kindness
  • embrace the path of Learn, Heal, Share, Experience/Emote/Express and Yield More

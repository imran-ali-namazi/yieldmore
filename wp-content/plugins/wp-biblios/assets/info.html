What is YieldMore

A place for all humanity to learn and grow, pooling their learning and resources. A website to organise and coordinate this. Anyone with an open mind, peaceful nature and a quest for progress is welcome.
  • a web repository of information and ideas worth learning and sharing.
  • a platform to publish selections from books and entire works
  • a place where contributors can share their body of work and views on a topic.
  • a meeting place of quality minded people whom one can interact with OFFLINE, on social media and on our mailing list.
  • provides curated lists of movies, books etc to improve language and get exposed to multi-cultural thoughts.
We are always looking for Curators (make selections worth sharing), Contributors (participate in discussions and expand articles) and Conveners (coordinate activities). Contact us by mailing Imran (shasa@cselian.com).

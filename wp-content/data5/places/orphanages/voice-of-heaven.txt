The Children we support are from various places like slums and remote villages. These children grow up in difficult circumstances, suffer from deprivation, exploitation and neglect through no fault of their own and for reasons beyond their control. The children we admit in our home are full orphans, semi orphans, deserved, rag pickers children, Gypsy, Sick parents, mentally affected parents, blind parents and AIDS affected parents.

All of our children are supported academically throughout their educational life and are encouraged to realise their full development potential and active learning capacity. Each child receives guidance and advice on possible career paths and also what is required of them to achieve their chosen paths. After schooling, the children are directed to vocational training courses, Childrenand also given the opportunity to go to university through the extended sponsorship programme.
We believe that every child deserves its right to realise his or her dreams.

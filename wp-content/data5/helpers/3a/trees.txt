<h2>Siva Temple Trees</h2>

The plan is to plant multiple saplings. Some of theses trees can live upto 100 years. Most of the trees, plants and shrubs have medicinal value. The details can be found <a href="docs/Ammai Appar Agam - Siva Temple Trees.pdf" target="_blank">here</a>. These need to be nurtured for atleast 3 years. We plan never to cut these trees. Only fruits and produce will be utilized for medicinal value. Apart from these trees and plants, we will identify some shrubs which will be grown in the nursery. We will also have an in-house Siddha doctor who will from time to time identify new species of plants and start growing them.

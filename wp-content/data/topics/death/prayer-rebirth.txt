Dear God,

We pray for the soul of ___________, related to ___________.

May she have met the moment of death with calm and peacefulness and little or no discomfort, pain and trepidation.

May she have soon realized that she had passed away from us and not look back to us with sadness, attachment or regret.

May her short visit to the world between lives be full of courage, acceptance and daring.

May she not be lured into phantasmal worlds which are hard to come out of.

May she not be too harsh on herself in judgement of her past birth and her actions therein.

May her time in purgatory / heaven / hell be adequate and productive.

May she choose wisely her next birth and be granted your Grace.

Amen

Thousands of years of data illustrate that within solar cycles there are periods where humanity is at great unrest and times change. Russian scientist Alexander Chizhevsky has provided years of data that clearly illustrates how these solar cycles and cosmic rays effect human Consciousness (see Figure 2).

Within each 11-year cycle, Chizhevsky recognised 3 years of minimum excitability, 2 years of growth in excitability, 3 years of maximum excitability, and 3 years of a decline in excitability. Interestingly, we see specific characteristics for each period:


 
Minimum Excitability
Duration: 3 years
Characteristics: Lack of unity in the masses, Indifference in the masses, tolerance in the masses.
Major historical events: 5%

Growth in Excitability
Duration: 2 years
Characteristics: Originating of new ideas, push to centralize on idea, masses begin to unite.
Major historical events: 20%

Maximum Excitability
Duration: 3 years
Characteristics: Discovery of solutions, masses more impatient with status quo, masses unite, new leaders appear.
Major historical events: 60%

Decline in Excitability
Duration: 3 years
Characteristics: Masses push for final changes, things begin to calm, peace often is prominent.
Major historical events: 15%


 
We are currently in the 3rd period of maximum excitability. It is therefore not surprising that we have been seeing social action such as the Turkish, Egyptian and Brazilian uprisings during the year 2013.

YaAllah,

I depend on you. I have no power of my own.

When things are going well for me, I know they have come from you and that I have no power of my own.

In my trials I  rely on you.

In my temptations I trust in you.

My success is in your hands and so is my life.

I have no power of my own.

Ya Jabaar, Ya Kareem, I cannot do without you.

Save me from all evil and guide my going out and coming in, now and forever more.

Amen.

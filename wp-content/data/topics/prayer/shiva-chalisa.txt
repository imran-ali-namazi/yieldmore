<div class="transliteration">Jai Ganesh Girija Suvan
Mangal Mul Sujan
Kahat Ayodhya Das
Tum Dey Abhaya Varadan</div>

<div class="english">Victory to Ganesh son of Girija,
Who is the root cause of all that is good?
Ayodhya Das, you request for the boon of fearlessness.</div>

1,2
<div class="transliteration">Jai Girija Pati Dinadayala
Sada Karat Santan Pratipala
Bhala Chandrama Sohat Nike
Kanan Kundal Nagaphani Ke</div>

<div class="english">Victory to the consort of Girija,
Who is very kind to the oppressed,
You always bless the saints,
Wear the baby moon on forehead,
And wear the snake’s hood as ear studs.</div>

3,4
<div class="transliteration">Anga Gaur Shira Ganga Bahaye
Mundamala Tan Chhara Lagaye
Vastra Khala Baghambar Sohain
Chhavi Ko Dekha Naga Muni Mohan</div>

<div class="english">His body is fair and the Ganges flows from his head,
He wears a garland of skulls and smears ash on his body,
He wears the hide of lion on his waist,
And seeing his pretty form the Naga sages are enchanted.</div>

5,6
<div class="transliteration">Maina Matu Ki Havai Dulari
Vama Anga Sohat Chhavi Nyari
Kara Trishul Sohat Chhavi Bhari
Karat Sada Shatrun Chhayakari</div>

<div class="english">The darling daughter Of Maina,
On your left side adds to your beauty,
With trident in hand and wearing skin of lion,
You always destroy your enemies.</div>

7,8
<div class="transliteration">Nandi Ganesh Sohain Tahan Kaise
Sagar Madhya Kamal Hain Jaise
Kartik Shyam Aur Gana rauo
Ya Chhavi Ko Kahi Jata Na Kauo</div>

<div class="english">You are with Ganesh and Nandi,
And look like the lotus flower in the middle of the sea.
You have with you the dark Kartikeya and Ganesh,
And who can describe this great scene?</div>

9,10
<div class="transliteration">Devan Jabahi Jaya Pukara
Tabahi Dukha Prabhu Apa Nivara
Kiya Upadrav Tarak Bhari
Devan Sab Mili Tumahi Juhari</div>

<div class="english">When the Devas called for victory,
Oh Lord, you swept away their sorrow,
When Tharakasura filled them with troubles,
All the devas got your generous help.</div>

11,12
<div class="transliteration">Turata Shadanana Apa Pathayau
Luv Nimesh Mahi Mari Girayau
Apa Jalandhara Asura Sanhara
Suyash Tumhara Vidit Sansara</div>

<div class="english">With haste, you sent to them the six faced God,
Who within no time destroyed him?
Then you killed the asura called Jalandhara,
And your fame spread all over the world.</div>

13,14
<div class="transliteration">Tripurasur Sana Yudha Machai
Sabhi Kripakar Lina Bachai
Kiya Tapahin Bhagiratha Bhari
Purahi Pratigya Tasu Purari</div>

<div class="english">You then waged war against Tripurasura,
And with great mercy on all gods,
The king Bhagirath undertook great penance,
And you helped him to fulfill his vow.</div>

15,16
<div class="transliteration">Darpa chod Ganga thabb Aayee
Sevak Astuti Karat Sadahin
Veda Nam Mahima Tav Gai
Akatha Anandi Bhed Nahin Pai</div>

<div class="english">Then the Ganga came down leaving her house,
And your devotees sing your praise always.
Even Vedas tried to sing your praise,
But Oh, primeval limitless one,
It did not succeed in its effort.</div>

17,18
<div class="transliteration">Pragati Udadhi Mantan te Jvala
Jarae Sura-Sur Bhaye bihala
Mahadev thab Kari Sahayee,
Nilakantha Tab Nam Kahai</div>

<div class="english">Out of the churning of the ocean came the flame,
Of poison which made devas and Asuras greatly scared,
And then you as Mahadev swallowed the poison,
And then you were called the blue necked one.</div>

19,20
<div class="transliteration">Pujan Ramchandra Jab Kinha
Jiti Ke Lanka Vibhishan Dinhi
Sahas Kamal Men Ho Rahe Dhari
Kinha Pariksha Tabahin Purari</div>

<div class="english">Then Lord Ramachandra worshipped you,
And won Lanka and gave it to Vibheeshana,
He wanted to worship thee with thousand lotuses,
And Oh, Lord you decided to test him.</div>

21,22
<div class="transliteration">Ek Kamal Prabhu Rakheu goyee
Kushal-Nain Pujan Chahain Soi
Kathin Bhakti Dekhi Prabhu Shankar
Bhaye Prasanna Diye-Ichchhit Var</div>

<div class="english">Oh, Lord, you hid one of those lotus flowers,
And Lord Rama instead gave one of his lotus like eyes,
Seeing the great devotion, You Lord Shankar,
Were delighted and granted him the desired boon.</div>

23,24
<div class="transliteration">Jai Jai Jai Anant Avinashi
Karat Kripa Sabake Ghat Vasi
Dushta Sakal Nit Mohin Satavai
Bhramat Rahe Man Chain Na Avai</div>

<div class="english">Victory, victory to you limitless, deathless one,
Shower your grace on all, Oh omniscient one,
I am being troubled by evil thoughts always,
And I am distressed and have no peace of mind.</div>

25,26
<div class="transliteration">Trahi-Trahi Main Nath Pukaro
Yahi Avasar Mohi Ana Ubaro
Lai Trishul Shatrun Ko Maro
Sankat Se Mohin Ana Ubaro</div>

<div class="english">Help me, help me, I call you Lord,
And I request your blessing just now,
Bring your trident and kill my enemies,
And free me from all these sorrows.</div>

27,28
<div class="transliteration">Mata Pita Bhrata Sab Hoi
Sankat Men Puchhat Nahin Koi
Swami Ek Hai Asha Tumhari
Ai Harahu Ab Sankat Bhari</div>

<div class="english">I have mother, father, brothers, and others,
But when I am in distress nobody enquires,
Oh Lord, I have only you left as hope,
Please remove this heavy sorrow from me.</div>

29,30
<div class="transliteration">Dhan Nirdhan Ko Deta Sadahin
Arat jan ko peer mitaee,
Astuti Kehi Vidhi Karai Tumhari
Shambhunath ab tek tumhari</div>

<div class="english">Oh Lord You always give wealth to those who are poor,
And you wipe away their difficulties,
Except you, I do not know anyone else,
Oh Shambhunath, please hear my prayer to you.</div>

31,32
<div class="transliteration">Shankar Ho Sankat Ke Naashan
Vighna Vinaashan Mangal Karan
Yogi Yati Muni Dhyan Lagavan
Sharad Narad Shisha Navavain</div>

<div class="english">Oh Lord Shankar, you are the destroyer of sorrows,
And also remover of obstacles and giver of good,
Sages seers and great ones meditate on you,
And even Saraswathi and sage Narada bow before you.</div>

33,34
<div class="transliteration">Namo Namo Jai Namah Shivaya
Sura Brahmadik Par Na Paya
Jo Yah Patha Karai Man Lai
To Kon Hota Hai Shambhu Sahai</div>

<div class="english">Salutation, salutations, victory, and salutations to Lord Shiva,
Even Gods and Brahma could not describe you
He who with devotion recites this prayer,
Would, without doubt, be helped by Shamboo.</div>

35,36
<div class="transliteration">Riniyan Jo Koi Ho Adhikari
Patha Karai So Pavan Hari
Putra-hin Ichchha Kar Koi
Nischaya Shiva Prasad Tehin Hoi</div>

<div class="english">He who recites this with intense love,
Becomes rich by the grace of the lord.
He who desires for a Son,
Would get one surely by the grace of Shiva.</div>

37,38
<div class="transliteration">Pandit Trayodashi Ko Lavai
Dhyan-Purvak Homa Karavai
Trayodashi Vrat Kare Hamesha
Tan Nahin Take Rahe Kalesha</div>

<div class="english">He who brings the priest on the thirteenth day of moon,
And conducts fire sacrifice with devotion,
And observes the penance of the thirteenth moon,
Would get the peaceful mind and healthy body.</div>

<div class="transliteration">Dhupa Dipa Naivedya Charhavai
Anta Vasa Shivapur Men Pavai
Kahai Ayodhya Asha Tumhari
Jani Sakal Dukha Harahu Hamari</div>

<div class="english">He who worships him with light and incense,
Would at the end live in the city of Shiva,
Ayodhya Prasad wishes to you,
Long life and eternal bliss.</div>

39,40
<div class="transliteration">Nitya Nema kari Pratahi
Patha karau Chalis
Tum Meri Man Kamana
Purna Karahu Jagadish</div>

<div class="english">After doing the day's regular duty,
I recite these forty verses with devotion,
Oh Lord of the universe, please fulfill,
All the desires of my mind.</div>

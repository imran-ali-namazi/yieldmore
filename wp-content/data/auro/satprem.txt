"The end of a stage of evolution," announced Sri Aurobindo,'is usually marked by a powerful recrudescence of all that has to go out of the evolution.' Everywhere about us,we can see this paroxysmal shattering of all the old forms;our borders,our Churches,our laws,our morals are collapsing on every side...We are in transition to SOMETHING ELSE.The earth is not going through a moral crisis,but through an 'evolutionary crisis'...Unknown to ourselves,we are in search of the new being; we are right in the midst of a human revolution."

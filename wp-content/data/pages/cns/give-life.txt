After the success of our campaign, #GIVE 2 years back which raised over 20000 Pounds worldwide, we successfully sponsored 10 heart surgeries and 3 medical management to the Iraqi needy children suffering from congenital heart defects at Narayana Health City, Bangalore, India. 

Building on the success of our campaign, we have decided to concentrate on this area on an annual basis. Very few organisations are working on this area as it involves HIGH RISK. We have taken the first step and with your involvement, we can help alleviate their troubles. 

With the blessed month of #Ramadan upon us, we are pleased to announce the launch of our #GIVE 2018 campaign! The #GIVE campaign aims to encourage the spirit of generosity, in the footsteps of Imam Hussain (as). 

Today, an estimated 3.5 million Iraqi children are living in poverty, bearing the brunt of a faltering healthcare system, worsened by ongoing conflict. The children of Iraq are being punished for no fault of theirs. 

Let us all reach out to people across the world and join hands to help the children of Iraq irrespective of their colour and faith.

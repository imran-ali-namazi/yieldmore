The process of living a successful life is different from a spiritual life,in the sense,that the former depends on positive thoughts,and the latter on transcending all thoughts.The former is self evident.In spirituality thoughts imply a thinker,who is the limiting factor.That is what is implied by surrender.The whole thinks,and u remain a witness,until u blend inextricably with the whole.understanding the process and accepting it wholeheartedly,is the foundation of a spiritual life.(even to say the whole thinks is only a poetic notion).one sits silently and learns to enjoy the ride.that's the start of the fun.But letting go of one's control is the difficult part.

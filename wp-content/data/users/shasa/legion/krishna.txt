I believe in God and world peace. The soul is immortal. Out of ignorance people started having controversies and fights without knowing their real nature. Without understanding what they want, they run after vain pursuits. They chase happiness and finally they land in nothing. Without understanding the real values of life, they are after ephemeral pleasures and land in depression. In order to understand our real self, we have to study the life of great spiritual masters and absorb their teachings. To know what they have undergone and what they have reached. By reading the biography of these great gurus and masters, we not only change our way of thinking, but our way of understanding people and our actions towards them becomes more harmonious.

Another way to explain what the Good Country is and how it works is by letting people experience it first-hand. We’ve designed a 2-3 hour, 30-300 person workshop that simulates how the Good Country identifies the issues it will tackle and the actions it will carry out. If you are interested in running this workshop at your university or office, please contact us at ambassadors@good.country and we will share the workshop materials with you.

Workshops may be run independently or, if they’re big ones, may be moderated by Madeline or Simon.

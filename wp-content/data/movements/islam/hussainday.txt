HUSSAIN DAY, Bangalore is an annual convention organised in order to bring all communities on one platform, to protect the principles of humanity, maintain social justice, propagate brotherhood mankind, inculcate values, be tolerant towards each other and respect all religions.

Each year, eminent personalities across India from different walks of life speak about the significance of the Martyrdom of Imam Hussain(A.S.) in today's context.

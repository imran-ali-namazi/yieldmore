<h2>Foster Family - Aged and Orphans</h2>

We will also put up small Kutirs (dwellings) for these elders and children to live as one family unit. This will be near a clump of trees. The initial plan is to have 10 families and 20 kids. Cooking and Laundry will be centralized. 2 bedrooms, a study room and a bathroom.

The foster parents will bring up the children as their own, inculcating our culture and guiding them in all aspects of life, within the guidelines set by Ammai Appar Agam. The parents will have certain daily routines to upkeep their health and mental alertness which will be guided through professionals at the Ashram.

The children will be given an all-round education as per the <a href="/3a/?node=gurukulam">Gurukulam</a> and the parents will give their utmost attention in the kids education and their personal upbringing.

The Sun is the reason that all life forms on Earth thrive. Why then, do we shy away from connecting and soaking up on the tremendous energies that direct contact, even for a few minutes, can offer?

I met a man years ago who was living on nothing but Sunlight. It was his seventh year and counting then! So vibrant and full of spark with no dearth of energy.

It's clear that our bodies are made up of the elements. With the introduction of shoes and heavily cemented spaces, we are losing touch with Fire and Earth.

Maybe today you could try and ditch that morning coffee and spend a few minutes in the sun.. or perhaps a barefoot walk in the lawn?

Thanks Chennai, for that extra Sun this morning!

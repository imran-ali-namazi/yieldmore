Thunderstorms and crispy sheets
waiting on a bed for one.
Eyes meet road. The radio is on.
Moonlight making stars
through the dew on my glass.

Forest songs in the air,
Mist in my hair.

Trees in random motion.
Their shadows, I watch in the dark.
A hasty knock on the door.
Loud giggles and hushed tones.

Me on my bed,
The dishes in the sink -
We both lay, undone.

Winter, you have finally come

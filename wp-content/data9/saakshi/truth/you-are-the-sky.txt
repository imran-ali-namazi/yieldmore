I was being shown around a Learning center in Coimbatore when this very beautiful wall (which you can see hidden behind the tree) caught my attention.

As I requested for permission to take a picture, the lady in charge explained to me that this special work of art was created by a boy with Autism.

With his bare hands, laying one stone after the other - entirely by himself. It seems that's all he ever did and loved to do

This just goes to show that to create, to learn, to do - children just need space, encouragement and simply just a chance!

Geniuses are not always discovered in words and equations.
Sometimes they are found in sculptures, in melody, in silence and on other occasions, they sit hiding in a WALL OF STONES

Dear child, you are not a soaring bird, you are the sky!!

And like someone rightly said, if your child lacks the skill to tie his own shoe laces, stop trying so hard and just give him some damn Slip-on shoes!!

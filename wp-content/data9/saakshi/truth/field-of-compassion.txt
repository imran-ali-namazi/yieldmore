We patronize the animals for their incompleteness, for their tragic fate of having taken form so far below ourselves. And therein we err, and greatly err. For the animal shall not be measured by man. In a world older and more complete than ours, they are more finished and complete, gifted with extensions of the senses we have lost or never attained, living by voices we shall never hear." ? Henry Beston

What is conscious living? It is a way of life where in you are mindful not only of your self, but you also ensure that the love and compassion you have is extended towards all life forms around you.

I'm sure none of my friends want to knowingly hurt any fellow beings. We use animals for races, carrying loads, keeping as pets, for entertainment, or to take a jolly ride, often without even thinking about the consequences of our action.

Do you really think that Divinity chose to experience itself as an animal, just to be raised in captivity by you - chained at home, locked up in cages, trained in stables and the likes? <br> Surprise answer: NO! They have a mind and spirit of their own and deserve to live wild and free, just as nature intended, even for you!

FACT: sitting on an elephant's back may cause permanent injuries, stunted growth, or organ failure as it's spine was never meant to take the added load of the humans and the social riding chairs. (Source- Nemanja pavlovic)

FACT: Animals are beaten up to be submissive and be trained for the riding pleasure of 'holiday makers' being forced to work in the tourist industry.

The taming process called 'the crush' especially in the case of elephants, involves killing the entire family except the kids and then torturing them to be obedient - (something I've even witnessed in schools today, minus the killing ofcourse).<br> I will spare you the horror of knowing the entire process.

FACT: Horses are ridden up to a state of exhaustion, whiplashed till finish lines, treated as commodities and then slaughtered or abandoned by 'owners' when they are 'done'. <br> How many times have you seen sickly horses on the road, finding it hard to even stand? In my case, many!

I am not even going to get started on the cruelty faced by dogs and other animals in the pedigree industry. If you are truly an animal lover, please research before you buy your fancy breed dogs. (If you're lazy let me summarise: THEY ARE TORTURED IN MOST CASES, I have seen with my own eyes).

And the situation with donkeys used as beasts of burden, birds being caged, dogs being chained etc., Are clear for you to see.

I CAN NEVER WRAP MY HEAD AROUND THE FACT THAT SOME PEOPLE THINK THEY OWN ANIMALS!! And that twice here, I was forced to use the word INDUSTRY to refer to an animal related situation.

Animals are just living beings like you, and not created for your use or pleasure.

Please. Extend your field of compassion! Let's be more aware.

Today, my friend texted me asking if I have experienced a Kundalini awakening.

Now, have I?

In my journey I have crossed paths and heard about Yogis who have had 'experiences' through their rigorous practices - acquiring powers, special abilities, Siddhis.

Folks sitting for hours, days, months, years and even lifetimes together in meditative silence and Sadhanas.

Why then have most of  these seekers not reached the goal?

Why are most Gurus sitting on pedestals and teaching with their inflated Egos?

Because most teachers don't impart the most basic and important foundation for their students who are seeking the Truth.

And what is that? Nothing but Humility, Gratitude and Respect.

In their lectures of complex philosophies about 'Dissolution of the Ego, the I' and 'Enlightenment' - they forget that the only cut for ego is HUMILITY!

Also my dear friends, as you sit for meditation, once in a while, open your eyes to see if there is a fellow brother seeking out for your loving kindness.

As you bend your bodies in complicated asanas, pause to ask if you are really going beyond your body?

Once in while, as you sit engrossed in your texts of Vedas, Geetas and Puranas, acquiring knowledge of this vast Universe - stop to examine if you are living by the TRUTH or just getting lost in a game of words and false power.

So how do you achieve this Humility?

It's simple - you just ask for it! Bow down, request and give Gratitude to the Universe for the blessings.

And that is all you really need to do.

I left traditional schooling at grade nine and that was hands down the best decision I made in life. (Kudos to my parents, Anita Sharma and Vijay Sharma for having the courage).

It gave me time and space to have a deeper understanding of myself, explore my talents which may otherwise not have bloomed, had I continued with the daily drill of mechanical school.

Met new people, had beautiful experiences, and took up a life path that truly made my real self shine :)

Like someone rightly said, the world needs more <a href="/works/the-prophet/?find=peacemaker">peacemakers</a>, lovers, storytellers, healers and empaths.

Can we be done with this rat race already?

I'm not saying school is bad. I'm saying it's very very bad.

Let's go back to the Forests

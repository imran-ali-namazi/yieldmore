With yoga gaining mass popularity in the west and other countries, it, like any other form of practice, is getting corrupt by "creative" modifications. Sure, weight loss, fitness and good health are an attractive benefit of yoga, we must remember that they are no more than a side effect of the ultimate path that it leads to. Asanas are merely a fraction of the universe of yoga that help us sit longer in meditation and other yogic practices.

So, If you're getting too proud of your bends and stretches and advanced asanas, be reminded that "Touching your knees with your nose is not Hatha Yoga, even a clown can do that and backwards, but that doesn't make him a yogi" - Swami Veda.

Maybe it will help to ask yourself every morning before practice about

How mindful you are

How present you are

If you are in a state of surrender

And then sit down to generate love with your Sadhana. Then simply share this love with all :D

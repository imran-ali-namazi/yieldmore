<h3>Eye Catcher</h3>
Teacher writes on the blackboard:
                                                   10000 = 1

<h3>Activity</h3>
Children cut figures of circles, squares, triangles
Write one large letter in the figure

<h3>Material Needed</h3>
* Chart papers:  4 colours
* Sketch pens : One set
* 6 pairs of Scissors
* Blackboard
* Chalk
* Dust bin

<h3>Procedure</h3>
Chart paper distributed.      Figures (square, triangle, rectangle, circle drawn on black board as model) are cut.
Children write a large letter English or Tamil on the cut figure.  Teacher collects the figures and 
compares sizes of circles and says ….             BIG OR SMALL CIRCLE IS SAME.   Children repeat.
Compares triangles.                                           BIG OR SMALL TRIANGLE IS ….     Children say …. SAME
Compares  rectangles                                        BIG  OR SMALL  RECTANGLE  IS .. Children say …. SAME
Takes circles.    Compares colours                   RED OR YELLOW  CIRCLE IS CIRCLE   Children repeat.
Takes triangles.  Compares colours.               YELLOW  OR GREEN  TRIANGLE IS    Children say  TRIANGLE.
Mixes all figures.  At random takes two figures.   CIRCLE OR SQUARE PAPER IS PAPER.   Children repeat.
Shows letters inside the figures and says      RED OR BLUE  LETTERS ARE …           Children say   LETTERS.
                                                                               BIG OR SMALL LETTERS ARE . . .       Children say   LETTERS.
                                INSIDE A TRIANGLE OR INSIDE A SQUARE LETTERS ARE . . .	     Children say   LETTERS.
Teacher says:        INSTEAD OF PAPER SHALL WE TAKE PEOPLE?
                                                            		BIG OR SMALL PEOPLE ARE PEOPLE.  Children repeat.
Teacher shows keeps right hand down and left hand up.  Children say  TALL OR SHORT PEOPLE ARE PEOPLE.
Teacher shows colours.  Children say             WHITE OR BLACK PEOPLE ARE PEOPLE.
Teacher encourages children to frame more contrasts.

<h3>Message</h3>
Differences in size, colour etc., do not matter when we see the commonality.  Children find that big or small numbers are numbers only!

<h3>Game</h3>
Children are divided into four groups.   Asked to stand facing North, South, East and West (Not looking at each other.)   They are shown same figure.   Some students see the colour of the chart and some see the colour of letter written inside.   When asked what colour did you see, they say differently.   Teacher asks, “INSIDE COLOUR OR OUTSIDE COLOUR?”   Children laugh and disburse.

<h3>Homework</h3>
Children are asked to find:
1. Differences between them and their father.     Also similarities.
2. Differences between them and their mother.     Also similarities.
3. Differences between them and their sibling/ friend.     Also similarities.

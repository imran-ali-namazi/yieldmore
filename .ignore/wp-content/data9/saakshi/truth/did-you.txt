Did you?

Did you hear the stranger sounds
from the holes you dug into the grounds?
Where stars hung thin from threads of smoke
Did you walk those roads of wispy hopes?
Kiss the night as it fell
onto your lap; did you tell -
the moon to play your favourite song?
Ask the angels to sing along?

Did you stop and watch upon
the love that paints the setting sun?
Sit beneath the trees in haze
and watch the dust misbehave?
Or let those fingers come to meet
an aching heart who was in need
of the gentle balm that trickles down
from your chest to turn that frown.

Did you pause to close your eyes
and touch the love that fills the skies?
The love that dances with the winds
and that which shimmers in your eyes.

The waters call you out to play
Perhaps your heart you must allow
To tumble with the leaves that sway
Oh my love, the time is now :)

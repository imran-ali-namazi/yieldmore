Why you should go Barefoot:

Relieves stress and pain while boosting the immune system.

There are antidepressant microbes in the soil which activate the release of brain serotonin.

For most of humanity, man was one with his primordial connection. Walking barefoot across the land, our bodies were continually linked to this massive energy field.

Now, thanks to the Advent of Asphalt and plastic, we have become increasingly separate from the Earth and it's many gifts.

We are endlessly emitting vibrational signals from our bodies while also receiving them from the objects around us.

Electrical currents transmitted from the Earth increases electrical charges, emitting electrons that neutralise diseases and inflammation (studied at the University of Arizona).

When our skin comes into contact with the ground, the molecules in our body receive an extra boost of vital energy. (Source - Gaia)

From a health standpoint the most fundamental benefit derives from the fact that being barefoot is the way nature intended so gives our feet the optimal neural feedback from skin contact with the ground.

Basically, being barefoot gives our brains more information for optimising balance and postural integrity. Muscle activity in our feet, legs and backs are better coordinated. Joint movements in our lower-limbs are enhanced. (Source- Cornwall podiatric).

Being barefoot is human. It is the most fundamental means of human movement. Tragically, over the thousands of years of using shoes we have crippled what was once elegant and natural into something stiff and awkward, with footwear which pays more attention to vain fashion than natural function.

No wonder humans require so much podiatric medical treatment. It is no accident that podiatry was invented after shoes. My profession spends the vast majority of its time treating the foot, leg and back problems caused as a direct result of wearing shoes. 

Humans have been wearing shoes for so long that most of us now believe we can't walk without them. Shoes have created a dependency. Most people don't think they have a choice of whether or not to wear shoes to walk or run. It's just a given.

Sadly for some, there is no longer a choice because they have truly become physically dependent on their footwear to the point where their muscles and joints can no longer function to support them as they walk or run.

For others, there is a choice, if only they can realise their supposed need for shoes is only psychological and they can actually ditch the artifical support and gradually regain the natural foot function they lost with careful rehabilitation of skin tone, muscle strength, joint movement and neural feedback. Once again achieving their optimal human movement.

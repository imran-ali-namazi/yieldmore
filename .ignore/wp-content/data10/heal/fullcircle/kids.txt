Full Circle is creating a unique experience for kids through a 5-day winter camp. We involve distinctive methods for holistic development through activities, self-assessment, individual feedback, games, and experiments, with hands on learning and interaction. Hop on to take them through a journey of empowerment by understanding themselves and allowing in happiness. 

Brief glimpse of topics: Self-discovery, Powerful communication and Self-expression, Creative thinking, Happiness - Empathy and Gratitude project, breaking stress through Art and Movement Meditation, Self-love, Grasping life, Confidence and Assertiveness.

Also: Art and craft, Fireless cooking, Fun games, Talent building and Party time!

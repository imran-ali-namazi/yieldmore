We have conflicting thoughts which create <a href="https://auromere.wordpress.com/2009/03/23/mental-formations-powers-and-perils/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">thought formations</a> that linger in our sub-conscious. If we keep feeding in negative thoughts, these formations become stronger and change the very tone of our thinking.

Positive in, negative out - in this manner, we can change our perception of the world around us. It's beautiful if only we learn to "<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GC0-WPjtMDs" rel="noopener" target="_blank">accept the good</a>" and "<a href="[url]wp-content/data/print/poster.jpg" rel="noopener" target="_blank">omit the negative</a>".

We encourage every person to discover an <strong>inner tranquility</strong> that could come from gardening, healthy discussion, <a href="[url]practices/prayer/?node=peace">prayer</a>, <a href="[url]practices/meditation/?node=chakras">meditation</a>, <a href="[url]songs/">music</a>, <a href="http://media.yieldmore.org/sanskrit/imran/asatoma-sad-gamaya/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">chanting</a>, physical exercise or simply sitting with <a href="[url]works/essays-on-the-gita/?find=sattwa">a good book</a> or lying next to <a href="[url]incubate/essays-to-a-swan/?node=mother">a loved one</a>.

Whatever brings us peace, we should seek to create an opportunity for it to manifest in our daily lives, for it is a very basic need of re-creation. Purging the negative is difficult when we are so used to hearing and reading about it and dwelling on it. What we need is to find an inner source - sometimes based on faith - of positivity and dynamic action. This doesn't happen in a day. We suggest slowly filtering out negative news and this cluttered and overwhelming overload of information.

Our brain has 2 sides - the left and the right. One works by logic and one by intuition. One develops skill, the other art / craft. What we need is to develop skillful art and artful skill (depending on which hemisphere is more dominant when we begin the <a href="https://www.monroeinstitute.org/node/1071" rel="noopener" target="_blank">process of balancing them</a>). Any ambidextrous activity like swimming or <a href="[url]practices/yoga/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">yoga</a> is one step in this direction. There are <a href="[url]programs/brainsync" rel="noopener" target="_blank">programs</a>, exercises and <a href="[url]chakras-app" rel="noopener" target="_blank">activities</a> that help to synchronise / balance the hemispheres.

Positive, balanced thinking is the need of the hour and cannot be achieved if we do not find an inner source of strength and a constructive path to action - to win our dreams, whatever they may be. I say to you, be creative and bold in your dream selection and begin with a positive <a href="[url]heal/practices/affirmations/">affirmation</a>, <a href="[url]speak/imran/?node=affirm">like mine</a>.

Dear friends,
We are creating a school!
<strong>Ankuram - A Shishu Mandir</strong>
A unique <strong>Temple for Children</strong> dedicated purely towards their <strong>innocence</strong>.

<strong>What is the Ankuram way?</strong>
- Respect towards all that one sees and senses
- Gratitude for all that one comes across in life.
- Being a Good, Caring, Compassionate human, above anything else.

<strong>Why this school?</strong>
We have gone too far away from Nature and in turn, our True selves!

<strong>What kind of school is this going to be?</strong>
- Self sustained and integrating the natural surrounding: from which the children observe and learn
- A space that includes and respects each and every kind of child
- Where the children are given the freedom and space to express themselves, allowing their <strong>inner true self</strong> to bloom.
- They explore the <strong>Universe</strong> outside and that within
- Completely experiential learning at individual pace.
- Where Innocence and curiosity are nourished

<strong>Our Foundation</strong>
Self-exploration, Simplicity, Freedom, Creative expression and abundance of LOVE

<strong>Teachers</strong>
- are so full of Awareness, Respect, Patience, Love and understanding of the uniqueness of a child!

<strong>As a result</strong>
- Children are facilitated to lead a <strong>deeper</strong> and more <strong>meaningful life</strong> full of <strong>Self-awareness</strong>, independent and <strong>free-thinking</strong>, and <strong>Conscious action</strong>.

What we love at Ankuram:
- Getting our hands dirty.
- Understanding each other and <strong>Laughing</strong> out loud.
- Truly learning through <strong>experience</strong> and <strong>insight</strong>.
- Keeping the <strong>wonder</strong> in children alive.
- <strong>hygiene</strong> of the self and surroundings.
- Independence, appreciation and kindness.
- <strong>Farming</strong>, star gazing, day dreaming, playing and creating.
- Being one with <strong>Nature</strong>

<strong>Where literature and love happen under one roof.</strong>

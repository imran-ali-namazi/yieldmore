The Bihar School of Yoga was founded in 1963 by <a href="http://www.biharyoga.net/yoga-vision/sri-swami-satyananda-saraswati/" target="_blank">Swami Satyananda Saraswati</a> and is currently led by <a href="[url]practices/yoga/?node=swan">Swami Niranjanananda Saraswati</a>. Satyananda Yoga Centre Mambalam has been taking free yoga classes for over 150 batches.

We have the following centres:

Classes go from Mon to Saturday for 3 weeks and an optional advanced pranayama class in the 4th week. With class introductions on a Sunday and other Sundays as contact class for old students. <a href="./?node=gallery">Sannyasi Krishna Yogam</a> is the Guru.

For the benefit of students, weekly Havan is conducted at the Ashram in Mambalam. Address can be had by writing to syc@satyamyogatrust.net.

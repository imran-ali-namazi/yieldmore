Welcome,

We help organize various activities in schools. With teachers day coming up on Sep 5th in India, the <a href="http://dir.yieldmore.org/jssw/" target="_blank">Journal of School Social Work</a> is trying to get children to send their favourite teacher a postcard. For more details, click <a href="/programs/school/?node=postcard">here</a>.

We also canvas for the <a href="http://blinkfoundation.org" target="_blank">Blink Foundation</a> that conducts an interesting change class in schools where college volunteers (Change Agents) look to do away with social differences and promote acceptance, harmony and unity.

For schools, there is also the <a href="/programs/school/pledge-festival">Pledge Festival</a> organized every Constitution Day (26th November) by the <a href="http://buildindiagroup.org" target="_blank">Build India Group</a>.

YieldMore, in 2015, compiled a <a href="http://english.yieldmore.org/songs/school/" target="_blank">list of songs</a> (non romantic) to be shared with children. You can also find some on <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RwUGSYDKUxU&list=PLsuI89eMBnMGa5CNzT7g0HHBRfkFjnVH6" target="_blank">our youtube channel</a>.

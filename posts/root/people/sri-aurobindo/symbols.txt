<p>The descending triangle represents Sat-Chit-Ananda. The ascending triangle represents the aspiring answer from matter under the form of life, light and love.</p>

<p>The junction of both - the central square - is the perfect manifestation having at its center the Avatar of the supreme - the lotus</p>

<p>The water inside the square represents the multiplicity, the creation</p>

<p>The four petals represent the four powers of the Mother</p>

<p>The twelve petals represent the twelve powers of the Mother manifested for Her work</p>

Shasa is the pseudonym of me, <a href="/imran-doc" target="_blank">Imran</a>. An African bushman (San) word for Good Water, Michel Shasa de Thiry Courtney is a character from <a href="http://wilbursmithbooks.com/books/courtney/the-burning-shore" target="_blank">The Burning Shore</a> and <a href="http://wilbursmithbooks.com/books/courtney/power-of-the-sword" target="_blank">Power of the Sword</a>.

Essays to a Swan was conceived circa 2013 though some of its essays are older.

Swan, for that is the translation of Hamsa, the ‘royal swan’ of the soul that floats in the cosmic ocean, beholding both its body and the ocean as manifestations of the same Spirit. And its also a play on Soham.

My older work can be seen <a target="_blank" href="/incubate/essays-to-a-swan/_pre2016/">here (pre 2016)</a>. Divine, Dear Brother and Charted are some of my most inspired ones.

In 2016, I began writing short pieces of blank verse and in 2017, to make them rhyme. These are listed below. Prose I began writing again in 2017 August and these can be found <a href="/speak/imran">here</a>.

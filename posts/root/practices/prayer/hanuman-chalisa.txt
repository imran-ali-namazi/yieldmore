Introductory Doha 1	
<div class="translit">Shreeguru charana saroja raja nija mana mukura sudhaari
baranau raghubara bimala jasu jo daayak phala chaari</div>

<div class="translation">"Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust from the Lotus-feet of Divine Guru, I describe the unblemished glory of Lord Rama, which bestows four fruits of Righteousness (Dharma),  Wealth (Artha), Pleasure (Kama) and Liberation (Moksha)</div>

<div class="words">ShreeGuru=revered Guru; Charana=feet; Saroja=lotus; Raja=dust/ particle; Nija=Mine;mana=mind;mukura=mirror; sudhaari=Cleansing/applying; Baranau=Describe; Raghubara=Of Raghu Vamsha, Lord Rama; Mala=impurity, Bimala=pure; Jasu=Glory; Jo=which, Daayak=bestower, Phala=fruit, Chaar=four</div>

Introductory Doha 2	
<div class="translit">Buddhiheena tanu jaanikai sumirau pavanakumara
Bal buddhi bidya dehu mohi harahu kalesa bikara</div>

<div class="translation">Considering this person as intelligence less, I remember Lord Hanuman. Give me strength, intelligence and knowledge, cure my body ailments and mental imperfections</div>

<div class="words">BuddhiHeena=without intelligence; tanu=body, person; jaanikai=knowing; sumirau=remembder; pavanakumar=son of wind god, Hanuman; Bal=strength; Buddhi=intelligence; Bidya=knowledge; dehu=give; harahu=remove, clear; kalesa=ailments; bikara=imperfections</div>

1	
<div class="translit">Jaya hanumana Jnaana guna saagara
Jaya kapeesha tihu loka ujaagara</div>

<div class="translation">Victory to Hanuman who is the ocean of Wisdom and Virtues,  Victory to the king of Monkeys who is illuminating three worlds</div>

<div class="words">Jaya=victory/ glory; Jnaana=Wisdom; guna=virtues/ qualities; saagara=ocean; Kapeesha=King of Monkeys=Hanuman; tihu=three; loka=world; ujaagara=illuminator</div>

2	
<div class="translit">Rama duta atulita bala dhama
Anjani putra pavanasuta nama</div>

<div class="translation">You are the messenger of Rama (to Sita), You are the abode of incomparable power. You are also called by the names of 'Anjani Putra' (Son of Anjana) and 'Pavana suta' (son of wind god)</div>

<div class="words">Doota=messenger; tulita=measured; atilita=immeasurable; bala=power; dhama=abode; Anjani=of Anjana; putra=son; pavana=wind; suta=son; naama=name</div>

3	
<div class="translit">Mahabira bikrama bajarangi?
kumati nivara sumati ke sangi</div>

<div class="translation">Oh mighty valorous one, of terrific deeds whose body organs are as strong as Diamond (or the weapon of God Indra). Cure my bad mind oh companion of those with pure (good) mind</div>

<div class="words">Maha=great;Beera=Brave; Vikram=great deeds; bajra=diamond; ang=body parts; kumati=bad intellect; nivara=cure, clean, destroy; sumati=good intelligence; ke=of; sangi=companion</div>

4	
<div class="translit">Kaanchana barana biraja subesa
kanana kundala kunchita kesha</div>

<div class="translation">"You are golden colored, you are shining in your beautiful attire. You have beautiful ear-rings in your ear and curly hairs</div>

<div class="words">Kaanchana=golden; barana=hue; biraja=resplendent, shining; subesa=good attire/ good looks; kanana=ear; Kundala=ear-rings; kunchita=curly; Kesha=hair</div>

5	
<div class="translit">Hatha bajra au dhvaja birajai
kandhe munji janeu sajai</div>

<div class="translation">"Vajrayudha (mace) and flag are shining in your hand. Sacred thread made of Munja grass adorns your shoulder</div>

<div class="words">Hath=hand, Bajra=Mace as powerful as vajrayudha or diamond; au=and; dhvaja=flag; biraji=take place; kaandhe=on shoulders; munji=of munja grass; janeoo=upavita thread, sacred thread; Sajai=adorn</div>

6	
<div class="translit">Shankara suvana kesari nandana
Teja pratapa maha jaga bandana</div>

<div class="translation">"O partial incarnation of Lord shiva, giver of joy to King Kesari. Your great majesty is revered by the whole world</div>

<div class="words">Shankara=Lord shiva; Kesari=King Kesari, father of Hanuman; Nandana=son. joy giver; Teja=shine, grandness; Pratapa=prowess; Maha=great; jaga=world; bandana=worship</div>

7	
<div class="translit">Bidyavana guni ati chatura?
Rama kaja karibe ko atura</div>

<div class="translation">"Oh one learned in all Vidyas, one full of virtues, Very clever. You are always eager to do Rama's tasks</div>

<div class="words">Bidyavan=one who has learned Vidyas; Guni=having gunas; ati=very; chatura=clever; Kaaja=task, work; Karibe=doing, do; ko=to; Aatura=eager</div>

8	
<div class="translit">Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiya
rama lakhana sita mana basiya</div>

<div class="translation">"You enjoy listening to Lord Rama's story; Lord Rama, Lakshman and Sita reside in your heart</div>

<div class="words">Prabhu=lord; Charitra=story, history;  sunibe=hear; ko=to; Rasiya=joy; Mana=mind; Basiya=reside, stay</div>

9	
<div class="translit">Sukshma rupa dhari siyahi dikhava
bikata rupa dhari lanka jarava</div>

<div class="translation">"Assuming the smallest form you saw (visited) Sita. Assuming the gigantic form you burnt down the Lanka</div>

<div class="words">sookshma=micro, minute; roopa=form, body; dhari=assuming, taking; siyahi=Sita; Dikhava=saw; Bikata=enormous; Lanka=Sri Lanka; Jaraava=burned</div>

10	
<div class="translit">Bhima rupa dhari asura samhare
ramachandra ke kaja samvare</div>

<div class="translation">"Assuming a terrible form you slayed demons. You made Lord Rama's works easier</div>

<div class="words">Bheema=terrible; roopa=form; Dhari=assuming; Asura=demon; samhar=destroy; Ramachandra=Rama of Chandra Vamsha; Kaja=work; Samvare=manage, make it easy, carry out</div>

11	
<div class="translit">laya sanjivani lakhana jiyae
shri raghubira harashi ura laye</div>

<div class="translation">"You brought Sanjeevini mountain to save Lakshmana's Life. Lord Rama embraced you in joy</div>

<div class="words">laaya=brought; Sanjeevani=a herb that brings back the dead; Lakhan=Lakshman; Jiyaye=saved; Raghubira=Brave one of Raghu Clan, Lord Rama; Harashi=with joy; Ura=neck; Laye=gave, brought</div>

12	
<div class="translit">Raghupati kinhi bahut badai
tum mama priya bharata hi sama bhai</div>

<div class="translation">"Lord Rama praised you very much saying 'You are dear to me like my brother Bharata'</div>

<div class="words">Raghupati=King of Raghu Clan; Bahut=very much; Badaayi=praised; Tum=you; mama=my, mine; priya=dear; Bharata=brother of Rama; hi=like; sama=equal; Bhai=brother</div>

13	
<div class="translit">sahasa badana tumharo jasa gavai
asa kahi shripati kantha lagavai</div>

<div class="translation">"'May the thousand headed serpent Adishesha sing of your glory' saying this Lord Rama embraced you</div>

<div class="words">Sahasa=thousand; badan=body; Tumharo=your; Jasa=success, glory; Gaavai=sing; asa=like this; kahi=saying; shripati=husband of Goddess shree or Lakshmi, Lord Rama; Kantha=neck; Lagavai=embrace</div>

14	
<div class="translit">Sanakadika brahmadi munisa
narada sarada sahita ahisa</div>

<div class="translation">"Sanaka, Brahma and other Royal sages, Narad, Saraswati and Adishesha</div>

<div class="words">Sanaka=sage sanaka, adika=other more; brahma=Creator Brahma; Aadi=and others; Muneesha=Royal sages; Narada=Sage Narad; Sharada=Goddess saraswati; Sahita=including; Aheeshaa=Adishesha</div>

15	
<div class="translit">Yama kubera dikpala jaha te
kavi kobida kahi sakai kaha te</div>

<div class="translation">"Yama, Kubera, Dikpaalakas, poets and singers; they can not describe your greatness properly</div>

<div class="words">Yama=God of Time/death; Kubera=God of treasures; Dikpaalas=Gods of 8 directions; Kavi=poet; Kovida=singer; Kahi=how; Kaha=say;</div>

16	
<div class="translit">Tuma upakara sugrivahi kinha
raam milaya rajapada dinha</div>

<div class="translation">"You helped Sugreeva. You made him friends with Rama which gave him his Kingship back</div>

<div class="words">Tuma=you; Upakaara=help; Sugreeva=Monkey King Sugreeva; Kinha=did; Milaaya=made them meet, join; Rajapada=kingship; Dinha=gave</div>

17	
<div class="translit">Tumharo mantra vibhishana mana
lankeshvara bhae saba jaga jana</div>

<div class="translation">"Vibheeshana accepted your Suggestion. He became the king of Lanka because of your advice, whole world knows it</div>

<div class="words">Tmharo=your; mantra=words; Vibheeshan=brother of Raavan who fought on Rama's side; Maana=accepted; Lankeshvara=King of Lanka; Saba=all; Jaga=world; Jaana=knows</div>

18	
<div class="translit">Yuga sahasra yojana para bhanu
lilyo tahi madhura phala janu</div>

<div class="translation">"You flew towards the sun who is thousands of years of Yojanas away, thinking of him as a sweet fruit</div>

<div class="words">Yuga=year; Sahasra=thousand; Yojana=distance of 10-15km, 8 mile is the most agreed upon distance; para=away; Bhanu=sun; Madhura=sweet; Phala=fruit; Janu=knowing, thinking</div>

19	
<div class="translit">Prabhu mudrika meli mukha mahi
jaladhi langhi gaye acharaja nahi</div>

<div class="translation">"Putting the ring of Rama in your mouth, you jumped and flew over Ocean to Lanka, there is no surprise in that</div>

<div class="words">Prabhu=Lord; Mudrika=ring; meli=in,over; Mukha=mouth; Mahi=keeping; jaladhi=Ocean; Laanghi gaye=jumped; Acharaja=surprise; Naahi=no</div>

20	
<div class="translit">Durgama kaja jagata ke jete
sugama anugraha tumhare tete</div>

<div class="translation">"All the difficult tasks in the world, become easy if there is your grace</div>

<div class="words">Durgama=difficult; Kaja=task; Jagata=word; Ke=of; Jete=how many; Sugama=easy; Anugraha=grace; Tumhare=your; tete=if there is</div>

21	
<div class="translit">Rama duare tuma rakhavare
hota na agya binu paisare</div>

<div class="translation">"Your the doorkeeper of Rama's court. Without your permission nobody can enter Rama's abode</div>

<div class="words">Duare=door;tuma=you;rakhavaare=keeper; hota=have, having; na=without; agyaa=permission; binu=nobody; paisaare=enter, come in</div>

22	
<div class="translit">Saba sukha lahai tumhari sarana
tuma rakshaka kahu ko darana</div>

<div class="translation">"All happiness stay with those who take refuge in you. You are the protector, why be afraid?</div>

<div class="words">saba=all; sukha=happiness, pleasures; Lahai=stay; tumhari=in your; sarana=refuge; tuma=you; rakshaka=protector; kahoo ko=why? or of whom; darana=be afraid</div>

23	
<div class="translit">apan tej samharo apai
tino lok hanka te kapai</div>

<div class="translation">"Only you can cancel your powers. All three worlds tremble in fear</div>

<div class="words">Aapan=your; Tej=power; Samharo=destroy, control; Apai=you; Tino=three; lok=worlds; hanka=fear; kapai=shake</div>

24	
<div class="translit">bhuta pishacha nikata nahi avai
mahabira jaba nama sunavai</div>

<div class="translation">"Evil Spirits and Ghosts don't come near when your name is heard O great Courageous one</div>

<div class="words">Bhoota=Evil spirits, Pishaacha=ghost; nikata=close; nahi=don't; avai=come; mahabira=maha+bira=great+brave; jaba=when; naama=name; sunavai=heard</div>

25	
<div class="translit">Nasai roga harai saba pira
japata nirantara hanumata bira</div>

<div class="translation">"Diseases will be ended, all pains will be gone, when a devotee continuously repeats Hanuman the brave's name</div>

<div class="words">Naasai=end, destroy; Roga=disease; Harai=end, close; Saba=all; Peera=pains, diseases, afflictions; Japata=keep repeating, remembering; nirantara=continuously; Beera=Brave</div>

26	
<div class="translit">Sankata te hanumana chhudavai
mana krama vachana dhyana jo lavai</div>

<div class="translation">"Hanuman will release those from troubles who meditate upon him in their mind, actions and words</div>

<div class="words">Sankata=troubles, difficulties; te=from; Chhudaavai=release; mana=mind; krama=actions; vachana=words; dhyana=meditate, contemplate; jo=who; Lavai=apply, do, bring</div>

27	
<div class="translit">Saba para rama tapasvi raja
tina ke kaja sakala tuma saja</div>

<div class="translation">"Rama is the king of all, he is the king of yogis. You managed all his tasks" or in other translation "He whoever takes refuge in Rama you will manage all their tasks</div>

<div class="words">Saba=all; para=on; Tapasvi=one of austerities; Raja=king; Tina ke=whose; Kaaja=work; tuma=you; saaja=carried</div>

28	
<div class="translit">Aura manoratha jo koi lavai
Soi amita jivana phala pavai</div>

<div class="translation">"Whoever brings many of their wishes to you, they will get unlimited fruits</div>

<div class="words">aur=many, more; Manoratha=mental wishes, desires; jo koi=whoever; Lavai=brings; amita=infinite; jivana=life; phala=fruits; pavai=get, receive</div>

29	
<div class="translit">charo juga pratapa tumhara
hai parasiddha jagata ujiyara</div>

<div class="translation">"Your glory is for all the four yugas, Your greatness is very famous throughout the world, and illumines the world</div>

<div class="words">Charo=four; juga=yugas; pratapa=glory; tumhara=your; hai=is; prasiddha=famous; jagata=world; ujiyara=illumined, spread</div>

30	
<div class="translit">Sadhu santa ke tuma rakhavare
asura nikandana rama dulare</div>

<div class="translation">"You are the guardian of Saints and Good people. You killed demons and you are dear to Rama</div>

<div class="words">Sadhu=good people, monks, simple people; Santa=saint; ke=of; tuma=you; Rakhavaare=keeper, guardian; Asura=demons; Nikandana=slayer; Dulare=dear</div>

31	
<div class="translit">Ashta siddhi nava nidhi ke data
asa bara dinha janaki mata</div>

<div class="translation">"Mother Sita granted you a boon to become the bestower of 8 Siddhis (supernatural powers) and 9 Nidhis (divine treasures)</div>

<div class="words">Ashta=eight; Siddhi=supernatural powers; Nava=nine; Nidhi=treasures; Ke=of; Daata=giver; Asa=like that; bara=boon; Dinha=give or gave; Janaaki=daughter of Janaka, Sita; Maata=mother</div>

32	
<div class="translit">Rama rasayana tumhare pasa
sada raho raghupati ke dasa</div>

<div class="translation">"You have the sweet devotion to Rama. May you always be a devotee of Lord Rama</div>

<div class="words">Ras=devotion, sweetness, love; Rasaayana=mixture or collection of sweetness; tumhaare=your; paasa=near; Sadaa=always; Raho= stay; Raghupati=Lord of Raghu Clan, Lord Rama; Ke=of; Daasa=servant, devotee</div>

33	
<div class="translit">Tumhare bhajana rama ko pavai
Janama janama ke dukha bisaravai</div>

<div class="translation">"Singing your name gets us Rama himself and Removes the sufferings of many lives</div>

<div class="words">Tumhare=your; Bhajana=chanting; Ko=to; Pavai=takes to, gives; Janama=life; Janama janama=life after life; Ke=of; dukha=unhappiness; Bisaravai=remove</div>

34	
<div class="translit">Anta kala raghupati pura jai
jaha janma hari bhakta kahai</div>

<div class="translation">"He who sings of you, at the end of the life he attains to Lord Rama's abode. Where he will be born as a Devotee of Lord Rama</div>

<div class="words">Anta=End; Kaala=time; Raghupati=Lord of Raghu clan, Rama; pura=city; Jaaee=go; Jaha=where; janma=born; Hari= Lord Rama; Bhakta=devotee; Kahai=called as, is said</div>

35	
<div class="translit">Aura devata chitta na dharai
hanumata sei sarva sukha karai</div>

<div class="translation">"Not contemplating on other gods, gets his all happiness from Hanuman by serving him</div>

<div class="words">Aura=more, other; Devata=gods; chitta=mind; na=dont; Dharai=contemplating; Sei=serving; sarva=all; sukha=happiness</div>

36	
<div class="translit">Sankata katai mitai saba pira
jo sumirai hanumata balabira</div>

<div class="translation">"Pains will be removed, all afflictions will be gone of who remembers Hanuman the mighty brave one</div>

<div class="words">Sankata=trouble; katai=cut short; Mitai=removed; Saba=all; Peera=pains, troubles; Jo=who; Sumirai=remembers; Bala=power; Bira=brave</div>

37	
<div class="translit">Jaya jaya jaya hanumana gosai
kripa karahu gurudeva ki nai</div>

<div class="translation">"Victory to you O master of the senses. Show mercy on us like a Guru does</div>

<div class="words">Jaya=victory; Gosai=master of senses; kripaa=mercy, compassion; karahu=do, show; guru=teacher, dispeller of darkness; Deva=god; Ki nai=like;</div>

38	
<div class="translit">Jo shata bara pathakar koi
chhutahi bandi mahasukha hoi</div>

<div class="translation">"He whoever recits this hundred times, his chains of Bondage will be cut, Great happiness will be his</div>

<div class="words">Jo=whoever; Shata=hundred; Baar=times; Paathakar=reciting; chhutahi=cut, removed; bandi=shackles, bondage; Mahasukha=great happiness, bliss; Hoi=happens, gets to</div>

39	
<div class="translit">Jo yaha padhai hanumana chalisa
hoya siddhi sakhi gaurisa</div>

<div class="translation">"He whoever reads these verses on Hanuman, he will get spiritual attainments, Lord Shiva is the witness to this statement</div>

<div class="words">Jo=who; yaha=this; Padhai=reads; Chalisa=40 lined hymn; Hoya=happens; Siddhi=attainments; Saakhi=witness; Gaureesha=Gowri+isha=Husband of Gowri=Lord Shiva;</div>

40	
<div class="translit">Tulasidasa sada hari chera
kijai natha hridaya mama dhera</div>

<div class="translation">"Tulasidas is always a disciple of Lord Rama. O lord make my heart your abode</div>

<div class="words">Sada=always; Hari= Lord Vishnu=Lord Rama; Chera=disciple, devotee; Kijai=please do; Natha=Lord; Hridaya=heart; mama=my, mine; Dheraa=abode</div>

Conclusive Doha	
<div class="translit">pavanatanaya sankata harana mangala murati rupa
rama lakhana sita sahita hridaya basahu sura bhupa</div>

<div class="translation">"O Son of wind god, remover of difficulties, oh one of auspicious form. With Ram, Lakshman and Sita  reside in our hearts of King of Gods</div>

<div class="words">Pavanatanaya=Pavana+tanaya=Wind+son, son of wind god, Hanuman; Sankata=trouble; Harana=remover; Mangala=auspicious; Murati=statue, form; Rupa=form; Sahita=including; Hridaya=heart; Basahu=reside; Sura=gods; Bhupa=king</div>

George Malley, a simple, small town, slow guy, sees a light the night of his 37th birthday and develops extreme intelligence and telekinesis. His projects and inventions highlight how creative man can be when he applies himself. This scene (see youtube link above) or quote below underscores why this movie's message is so important - that we firmly grasp life and find our true potential. And the closing credits song (see link above) wonderfully accents the urgency with which this message is made - "That this love I have inside Is everything it seems But for now I find It’s only in my dreams That I can Change the world". A powerful call to action, like <a href="http://yieldmore.org/books/curious-lives/">Curious Lives</a> and <a href="http://yieldmore.org/works/jonathan-livingston-seagull/">Jonathan Livingston Seagull</a>.

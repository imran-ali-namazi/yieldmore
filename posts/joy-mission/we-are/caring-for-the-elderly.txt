A core part of our Joypreneur / Joystreet program is care for the elderly. In this, we have Imran and Saify's flagship "Consciously Growing Older" program which focuses on the needs of 50+ year old people. Their relations with adult sons and daughters is strengthened in the 2 day workshop where estranged children / spouses are welcome on the 2nd day, after a small transformation is manifest.
